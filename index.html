Build your own French sentence

📍 Où est… ? — Where is…?

Choose an object, a place word, and another object to create your own sentence. Try sur (on), sous (under), à gauche de (to the left of), or à droite de (to the right of)!

Le chat est sur la table.

The cat is on the table.

Sentence pattern: Object + est + place word + object. For example: “Le livre est sous la chaise.”
